Diabetes Prediction System

What is Diabetes?

Diabetes is a chronic condition where the body has difficulty regulating blood glucose (sugar). Over time, high blood glucose can damage blood vessels and nerves, affecting the heart, kidneys, eyes, and more.

Types
  • Type 1: Autoimmune; minimal insulin production
  • Type 2: Insulin resistance; most common
  • Gestational: During pregnancy
Risk Factors
  • Family history, age, ethnicity
  • Overweight and inactivity
  • Gestational diabetes history
Why Predict Early?
  • Supports lifestyle changes
  • Enables early clinical guidance
  • Helps reduce long‑term complications

Professional Information

Transparency about methodology, privacy, and proper usage.

About the System

This application provides an AI-assisted diabetes risk assessment based on user-provided health parameters. It is designed to support awareness and education, not to diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease.

Model & Data
  • Supervised ML model trained on tabular health indicators
  • Inputs include glucose, blood pressure, BMI, age, and others
  • Periodic evaluation and iterative improvements
Privacy & Security
  • We minimize data and process inputs solely for risk estimation
  • No third-party sharing of entered values
  • Use strong passwords; log out on shared devices
Medical Disclaimer

The results are informational only and not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Performance Insights

Best Performing Algorithm

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) achieved the highest accuracy of 84.2% and is currently used in production.

  • High precision (86.1%) - low false positive rate
  • Good recall (81.3%) - catches most diabetes cases
  • Balanced F1-score (83.6%)
  • ROC-AUC of 0.842 indicates good discriminative ability
Model Characteristics

Instance-based learning approach that classifies based on similarity to training examples.

  • Non-parametric method - no assumptions about data distribution
  • Simple to understand and implement
  • Effective for small to medium datasets
  • Robust to outliers and noise

Data Quality & Validation

Dataset Quality

Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset - well-established benchmark dataset with 768 samples and 8 features

Cross-Validation

5-fold cross-validation used to ensure robust performance estimates and prevent overfitting

Class Balance

Dataset has 65% negative cases and 35% positive cases - reasonable balance for diabetes prediction

Important Considerations
Model Limitations:
  • Accuracy of 84.2% means ~16% of predictions may be incorrect
  • Model trained on specific population (Pima Indians)
  • May not generalize well to all ethnic groups
  • Requires regular retraining with new data
Recommendations:
  • Use predictions as screening tool, not diagnosis
  • Always consult healthcare professionals
  • Consider additional features for better accuracy
  • Implement model monitoring and drift detection
