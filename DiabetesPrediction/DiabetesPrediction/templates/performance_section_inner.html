Performance Insights

Best Performing Algorithm

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) achieved the highest accuracy of 84.2% and is currently used in production.

  • High precision (86.1%) - low false positive rate
  • Good recall (81.3%) - catches most diabetes cases
  • Balanced F1-score (83.6%)
  • ROC-AUC of 0.842 indicates good discriminative ability
Model Characteristics

Instance-based learning approach that classifies based on similarity to training examples.

  • Non-parametric method - no assumptions about data distribution
  • Simple to understand and implement
  • Effective for small to medium datasets
  • Robust to outliers and noise

Data Quality & Validation

Dataset Quality

Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset - well-established benchmark dataset with 768 samples and 8 features

Cross-Validation

5-fold cross-validation used to ensure robust performance estimates and prevent overfitting

Class Balance

Dataset has 65% negative cases and 35% positive cases - reasonable balance for diabetes prediction

Important Considerations
Model Limitations:
  • Accuracy of 84.2% means ~16% of predictions may be incorrect
  • Model trained on specific population (Pima Indians)
  • May not generalize well to all ethnic groups
  • Requires regular retraining with new data
Recommendations:
  • Use predictions as screening tool, not diagnosis
  • Always consult healthcare professionals
  • Consider additional features for better accuracy
  • Implement model monitoring and drift detection
